How to trim and compress a video on iPhone

Updated October 4, 2026 · 1 min read

Quick answer

In Compress Video tap Trim Video, drag the handles to keep only the part you need, tap Save, then tap Compress. Size scales with duration, so cutting a 60-second clip to 15 seconds removes about three quarters of it before compression even starts.

Photos can trim too, but the trimmed part keeps the original bitrate, so a 4K clip stays heavy. Trimming and compressing together gets you a small file in one pass.

Trim and compress a video

One app, one pass.

  1. 1

    Open the clip

    Pick the video in Compress Video.

  2. 2

    Tap Trim Video

    Drag the start and end handles; the selected length is shown above the timeline. Tap Save.

  3. 3

    Compress

    Open Compress settings, pick resolution, frame rate and audio, tap Compress.

  4. 4

    Save

    Check the new size and tap Download Compressed.

Questions people also ask

Can I undo a trim?

Yes. Tap Original to go back to the untouched clip; the source video is never changed.

Compress Video saves a new, smaller copy of your video. The original stays untouched until you delete it. Upload limits of third-party services change; check the service if a file is close to its limit.
