## Description (verbatim)
Compress Video shrinks a video file on your iPhone. Reduce size, trim, and export a smaller clip you can send or save. Processing stays on device. Try it now.

Compress Video is an iOS app for shrinking a video file on iPhone and iPad. It is a video file compressor: import a clip, optionally trim it, then export a smaller file you can save or share.

People use Compress Video when a recording is too large to send, too slow to upload, or is filling iPhone storage. Typical users are anyone with 4K camera-roll clips, screen recordings, or downloads that chat apps, email, and social uploads reject.

The problem is simple. Phone videos are huge. Deleting the only copy is not a good fix, and a full video editor is more than most people need.

Compress Video solves that job on the device. It re-encodes the clip (HEVC) with controls for resolution, frame rate, and audio so you can reduce size without guessing at bitrate presets. You see original vs compressed size before you save.

What this app does: use this app to compress a video file on iPhone without uploading it to a server.

FEATURES
- Import a video from Photos, Files, Share Sheet, or the Action extension
- Trim the timeline so you only keep the part you need
- Adjust resolution, frame rate, and audio (compress, keep, or mute)
- Compare original and compressed file size before you export
- Save the smaller file to Photos or share it
- Process the video on your iPhone or iPad — files are not uploaded for compression

HOW TO USE
1. Open Compress Video and pick a clip, or share a video into the app.
2. Trim unused footage if you only need part of the recording.
3. Choose how small the file should be (resolution, frame rate, audio).
4. Compress, then save to Photos or send the smaller file.

USE CASES
- Shrink a clip so it actually sends in chat or email
- Reduce video size to reclaim storage from 4K, slow-mo, and screen recordings
- Export a smaller file so uploads and backups finish sooner

PRIVACY
Encoding stays on your device. Compress Video does not upload your video to a server in order to compress it.

PRICING
The app is free to try, with a daily convert/share limit and one saved project. A premium subscription (in-app purchase) unlocks more than one project and removes the daily share limit.
