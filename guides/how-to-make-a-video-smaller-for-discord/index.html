How to make a video smaller for Discord on iPhone

Updated October 4, 2026 · 2 min read

Quick answer

Free Discord accounts can upload files up to 20 MB (raised from 10 MB in August 2026); Nitro Basic allows 50 MB and Nitro 500 MB. In Compress Video, trim the clip, drag the resolution slider down, lower the frame rate to 30 fps, tap Compress, and check that the Compressed size is under your limit. Then share it to Discord.

Gaming clips and screen recordings are exactly the videos that blow past Discord's limit: high resolution, 60 fps, long.

Settings that fit 20 MB

  • Clips under 30 seconds: around 1080p (50% of 4K), 30 fps usually fits.
  • 30–60 seconds: around 720p (about a third of 4K), 30 fps.
  • Longer: trim to the highlight, or move the slider lower still.

Questions people also ask

What is Discord's file size limit for free users?

20 MB per file since August 2026. Before that it was 10 MB.

Can I send a longer video on Discord without Nitro?

Yes, if you compress it under 20 MB, or trim it into a shorter highlight.
